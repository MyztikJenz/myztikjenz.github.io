<  Wood Cart

As I get a bit more into fiddling in the garage, I find myself running into the same problem a lot of other garage tinkerers run into: I’ve got too much wood. It’s everywhere, balanced on anything that can hold a half-sheet of plywood. And it got annoying.

Wading through a bunch of ideas on Pinterest, I finally came across something that looked like it would work. This cart by Fix This Build That looked simple enough that even a noob like me could build it. I bought the plans, modified them to shorten the overall length by a foot (the spot I wanted to put this would only fit a five foot cart), and got to cutting. I think it turned out OK.

However, I quickly realized I did indeed have a problem. Piling all my stuff onto the cart, it was already full on the sides. :/ And the shelves would ultimately get pretty full once I had all the smaller offcuts on it.

I should have expanded the floor width by an inch or two on both sides, would have afforded a lot more space of bigger sheets. Oh well, it’s a problem I can solve by actually using some of this stuff.
