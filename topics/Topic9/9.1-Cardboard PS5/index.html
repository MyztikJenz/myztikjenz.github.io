<  Cardboard PS5

I really wanted a PS5 for Christmas 2021. Alas, it didn’t show up until Jan 7th of the next year. No biggie, but my oldest saw fit to make sure I didn’t end up without a PS5 on Christmas day:

One of my most favorite gifts I’ve ever gotten.

But being the dork I am, I thought it’d be cool to mod it to include lights similar to the actual PS5. And to make it more a challenge, I used an ATtiny85. An 8-bit AVR microcontroller with a whopping 512 bytes of RAM and only 6 I/O pins. Given I was planning to use four RGB LEDs, some additional help was going to be needed.

Nothing that a little 74* logic can’t resolve. Two SN74HC595 ICs and we’re golden.

Getting everything wired was a bit more challenging. The space in the PS5 was limited and I needed the tiny to live on it’s own board, separate from the button, the USB connection, and the two LED boards. The wiring wasn’t complicated, just a bit to manage to get it all to align properly in the box.

I used two strips of plastic from a milk jug to diffuse the lights, which worked remarkably well.
