I first encountered Panic Park at California Extreme (CAX) several years ago and it was amazing. So much fun, including all the members of my family. Which was unique… not everyone likes every game. Except Panic Park.

The game isn’t terribly common but it was one that my wife said was OK to get if it ever did. That makes it “her game”, which I remind her of every time she notes how much space it takes up in the garage.

These things don’t show up very often for sale and when they do, it’s usually not a cheap proposition. I tried to get one during The Pandemic™ and got outbid. Which was for the best, getting this thing during lockdown would have been a mess. I eventually got lucky and picked up one from The Captain The auction was a bit intense… I was at a party, not entirely sober, screaming at the slow-rolling auctioneer through my laptop “Say ‘sold’ motherfucker! SAY IT!”. People around me were concerned.

The game “needed work” but I was happy to find out it was just an isolation transformer that was expecting 220V. Swapped it out for one that wanted 120V and now it works like a charm… but it did come with some interesting, uh, surprises.

Mark had some choice hand gestures for the tiedowns I’d had forgotten I even had. Using packing tape, while brilliant in my mind, ended up flying away on the I-5. The double bird was warranted.
