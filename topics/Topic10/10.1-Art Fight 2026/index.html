Art Fight is this site that encourages artists of all mediums to join teams and “attack” each other by creating other’s characters in their own style. It’s all in good fun and there seems to be a fairly robust and supportive community there.

I got there because my oldest is there. And I told them, “I should attack you!”. However, I have shit drawing skillz (in that I have none whatsoever). But there aren’t limits on what medium you can use… so just like the katana, I leaned into my child’s love of weaponry.

The character I picked was one they were asking people to attack and hadn’t yet. So it seemed to be the best candidate. The metal was cut at the Maker Nexus on their xTools MetalFab and I welded it myself… mostly correctly.

The bulk of the work was not in modeling, it was tracing a vector over the art. The name was the worst as I tried to do it by hand until I found there’s a one-click solution in Affinity 🤦🏼‍♂️. I also needed to strategically leave gaps so when the MetalFab cut it didn’t drop parts out.

You can check out my ArtFight profile here. Some folks attacked the dumb characters I put up and they did such a good job. I think I like this one the best.
